# SURYAVAYU AI

## Smart Renewable Energy and Agriculture Monitoring System

SURYAVAYU AI is a student innovation project focused on clean energy, smart agriculture, and rural development. The project combines solar energy, wind energy, rechargeable battery storage, and AI-based monitoring to support sustainable farming.

## Problem Statement

Many farmers and rural users face problems such as:

- Power shortage in farming areas
- High electricity cost
- Difficulty in monitoring field and energy conditions
- Lack of affordable smart agriculture tools
- Climate and environmental challenges

These problems can affect irrigation, agriculture productivity, and daily rural activities.

## Proposed Solution

SURYAVAYU AI is a beginner-friendly prototype that shows how renewable energy and AI can work together.

The system focuses on:

- Generating clean energy using solar power
- Generating additional power using wind energy
- Storing energy in a rechargeable battery
- Monitoring energy levels using a dashboard
- Giving AI-based recommendations for smart agriculture usage

## How It Works

1. Solar and wind energy values are shown in the dashboard.
2. Battery level is calculated based on available renewable energy.
3. The system analyzes the energy level.
4. AI logic gives a recommendation based on energy availability.
5. Farmers or users can use the suggestion for better energy planning.

## Project Dashboard

The website includes a live prototype dashboard with:

- Solar Power
- Wind Power
- Battery Level
- Farm Condition
- AI Recommendation

The dashboard values update automatically to simulate real-time monitoring.
