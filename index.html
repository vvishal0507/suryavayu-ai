SURYAVAYU AI

Smart Renewable Energy and Agriculture Monitoring System

SURYAVAYU AI is a student innovation project that combines solar energy, wind energy, rechargeable battery storage, and AI-based monitoring to support sustainable farming and rural development.

AI + Renewable Energy + Agriculture

A beginner-friendly prototype for clean energy generation and smart farm monitoring.

Problem Statement

Power Shortage

Many rural and farming areas face electricity shortage, which affects irrigation, monitoring, and daily agriculture activities.

High Electricity Cost

Farmers often depend on traditional electricity sources, which can increase farming expenses.

Lack of Smart Monitoring

Small farmers may not have access to AI-based tools for energy and agriculture decision-making.

Climate Challenges

Agriculture is affected by changing weather, energy demand, and environmental issues.

Proposed Solution

Solar Energy

A solar panel can generate clean energy from sunlight during daytime.

Wind Energy

A small wind turbine can generate power when wind is available.

Battery Storage

Generated energy can be stored in a rechargeable battery for later use.

AI Monitoring

AI logic can analyze energy level, weather condition, and farming needs to give useful suggestions.
